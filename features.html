Collection Management

Attesté Certificate

Living digital provenance with SHA-256 integrity hashing, tiered certification (Standard / Enhanced / Professional), QR codes, and shareable certificate faces. Your artwork's verified biography — which, for artist-made works, begins with its First Witness: the maker’s own account, recorded and sealed at creation.

AI & Intelligence

AI Art Sommelier

Claude-powered taste profiling across 8 aesthetic axes. Weekly discovery digests introduce you to artists you'll love but haven't found yet. Your personal art advisor.

Atelier — Voice & Vision Companion

The signature Attesté experience. Point your camera at a work and simply talk — Atelier sees what you see, answers aloud about style, technique and provenance, and saves every session as a journal entry pinned to the artwork. Text chat with full collection context included.
