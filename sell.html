Sell & get paid

Sell the work. Get paid. Skip the wire-and-pray.

A buyer you’ve never met wants the piece. You want to be paid before it leaves the studio; they want the work before they pay. Selling inside Attesté gets you both what you want: the buyer’s money waits with a licensed escrow service until the work arrives, then it’s released to you, and ownership and the certificate move to the buyer at the same time.

How it works

Three steps, and nobody has to go first on trust

1

Agree the sale

List the work and agree the price with your buyer in Attesté. The certificate and the work’s record are already there, so the buyer can check them before paying.

2

The buyer pays escrow

The buyer pays a licensed escrow service, not your personal account and not Attesté. The money waits there while you ship or hand over the work.

3

Delivered, released

When the buyer confirms the work arrived, the escrow service pays you, and the work’s ownership and certificate move to the buyer.

Where the money goes

The right way to pay, depending on where you sell

Attesté is the software that runs the sale. It never holds the buyer’s money: a licensed escrow service holds it, or it settles straight into your own merchant account.

Fees

The buyer pays the price. The seller pays the fees.

Attesté charges a 1% fee on sales paid through GoSafe escrow; the seller pays it, alongside GoSafe’s 1.5%, and the buyer pays the price.

If the seller is registered for VAT, the buyer pays the price plus VAT. GoSafe escrow is funded by PayShap or EFT; cards are not used for escrow.

Selling is open to galleries on any plan and to artists on a paid plan.
