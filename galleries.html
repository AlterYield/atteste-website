New · Talking Provenance

Every consigned work can speak.

For 500 years, gallery knowledge has lived in private letters and lost when galleries closed. Talking Provenance changes that. Record a Curator’s Commentary on works you’ve consigned or historically represented — market context, attribution, exhibition history. The recording is hash-verified, attributed to your gallery, and travels with the work for life. Institutional memory, finally on the record.

1

Curator’s Commentary

Walk a work in your space, microphone in hand. The Curator AI surfaces gaps in the record and probes for what only your gallery knows: exhibition history, attribution, market chronology. You answer; the system captures.

2

Your Gallery’s Voice on the Record

Every recording carries your gallery’s identity, hash-chained to the artwork. When the piece resurfaces at auction in twenty years, your gallery’s voice is part of the chain of custody — institutional memory turned into a permanent asset.

3

Cumulative Across Your Inventory

Record on currently consigned works and on works you’ve ever represented historically. Galleries that record consistently build a compounding portfolio — every artwork they’ve ever touched carries their voice forward.

Available on Professional and Enterprise tiers. Unlimited recordings per gallery account · up to 240 seconds per session · addenda welcome.
