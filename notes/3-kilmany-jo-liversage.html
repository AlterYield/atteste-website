‘PREKARIA’

Kilmany-Jo Liversage · @kilmany_jo
Read by the Attesté Atelier · 13 August 2026

Acrylic, aerosol and marker across 150×150 — and the contour drips stay raw, speed kept on purpose as evidence. ‘PREKARIA’ is signed under the graffiti alias ORDA: the street stays visible inside the gallery. The unfinished edge is the honesty.
