‘Kissing Studies’

Motlhoki Nono · @motlhoki_nono
Read by the Attesté Atelier · 16 August 2026

Monotypes made by kissing the plate wearing lipstick — the print matrix is the artist’s own mouth. In the companion piece, ‘The Weight of a Kiss’, a flatbed scanner stands as witness. Printmaking has always been pressure; ‘Kissing Studies’ makes the pressure tender.
