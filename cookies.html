1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. In addition to traditional HTTP cookies, modern web applications use other client-side storage mechanisms that serve similar purposes. These include:

  • IndexedDB — a browser database that allows applications to store structured data locally on your device.
  • localStorage — a key-value storage mechanism that persists data across browser sessions.
  • sessionStorage — similar to localStorage but limited to a single browser session.

Under the European ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC, as amended by 2009/136/EC), all of these technologies are treated equivalently because they involve storing and accessing information on your terminal equipment. Throughout this policy, when we refer to "cookies," we include all of these storage technologies unless stated otherwise.

2. How We Use Cookies and Local Storage

Attesté is a single-page web application built with Flutter and hosted on Firebase Hosting. The web apps set Google Analytics cookies today, without asking first (section 2.3). They use no cookies for advertising. Our marketing website (atteste.art) offers optional, consent-gated cookie categories (currently analytics and marketing, described in sections 2.3 and 2.4 below); none are set unless you explicitly opt in via the cookie banner. Our use of client-side storage falls into the following categories.

2.1 Strictly Necessary Storage

These storage mechanisms are essential for the application to function. They cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality. Under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, strictly necessary storage does not require consent.

Technology Purpose Duration
Firebase Auth (IndexedDB) Stores your authentication session token so you remain signed in between visits. Without this, you would need to log in every time you open the application. Persists until you sign out or the token expires (typically 1 hour, with automatic refresh).
Firestore Offline Cache (IndexedDB) Caches your collection data locally so the application loads quickly and can function during temporary network interruptions. Persists until you clear browser data or sign out. Cache size is managed automatically by Firebase.

2.2 Functional Storage

These storage mechanisms remember your preferences to provide a more personalised experience. They do not track you across websites or share data with third parties.

Technology Purpose Duration
Theme Preference (localStorage) Remembers whether you selected dark mode or light mode so your visual preference is applied immediately on each visit. Persists until you change your preference or clear browser data.

Your currency preference and country setting are stored in your user profile on our servers (Firestore), not in local storage. These are covered by our Privacy Policy rather than this Cookie Policy.

2.3 Analytics Storage

The Attesté apps. The web apps, including Galleria, set Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) when they load, without asking first, and this policy used to say they did not. On galleria.atteste.art those cookies are set for all of atteste.art. The mobile app keeps an analytics ID on your device in the same way. We are changing this: the web apps will stop using Google Analytics, and a mobile app update will ask first and do nothing unless you opt in. Section 2.9 of our Privacy Policy gives the detail.

The marketing website. On our marketing website (atteste.art), we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — but only after you explicitly opt in via the cookie banner. If you decline, or simply make no choice, Google Analytics never loads and no analytics cookies are set.

Google Ads. Our Google Ads account reads some Google Analytics events to measure whether our advertising works: enquiry forms sent and clicks on app-store buttons on the marketing website, and sign-ups and purchases in the web apps. Section 2.10 of our Privacy Policy gives the detail.

Technology Purpose Duration
Google Analytics (_ga, _ga_* cookies) Aggregated statistics about page visits and site usage on the marketing website. Set only after you opt in to the "Analytics" category. Up to 2 years (browser-dependent), or until you withdraw consent or clear browser data.

2.4 Advertising Storage (Marketing Website Only — Opt-In)

On our marketing website, we use the Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) to measure the performance of our Facebook and Instagram advertising and to build audiences for retargeting — but only after you explicitly opt in via the cookie banner. Declining is exactly as easy as accepting, and the default is off. If you do not opt in, the pixel never loads, no advertising cookies are set, and no data is shared with Meta.

Technology Purpose Duration
Meta Pixel (_fbp cookie) Records the pages you view, clicks on app-store links and on our main sign-up, demo and contact buttons, and, on our thank-you page, form-submission events, together with details from those pages (see below), so we can measure our Meta ads and show relevant ads to previous visitors on Facebook and Instagram. Set only after you opt in to the "Marketing" category. 90 days, or until you withdraw consent or clear browser data.

We do not add your name, email address or anything you type to these events. We do not use Meta’s "advanced matching" features. The pixel is not loaded on our free COA generator.

Automatic button and page data. If you accept, the pixel also automatically sends Meta which buttons you click, including their labels, and details that describe the page. If you click a form’s button, it also sends the form’s field names and which fields were left empty. It does not send what you type into a form.

Extra page details. If you accept, Meta may also use its AI to read details from the page you are viewing and send them with these events. That can include the page title and description, images, business name and location, event details, authors named on the page, product or service names, attributes, reviews and prices. Meta uses this to improve ad delivery, reporting and product listings.

Measuring ads on your device. On websites where people log in with an email address or phone number, Meta may check on the person’s own device whether an ad led to an action on the site. Meta says this uses details already shared with the site plus temporary device information, and that it receives only confirmation of the action and which ad to credit. Our pages that carry the pixel have no such login, so we do not expect this to apply here. If that changes we will update this policy.

We do not sell your data to advertisers. Beyond the consent-gated Meta Pixel described above, the only advertising service we use is Google Ads, which measures our advertising from Google Analytics events (section 2.3). We do not participate in real-time bidding systems. The Attesté apps show no advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

The only third-party services that may store data on your device are:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — provides authentication and database caching as described in sections 2.1 above. Firebase operates under Google's data processing terms. Data may be processed in the United States. For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — on the marketing website, only if you opt in to analytics cookies; in the Attesté apps as described in section 2.3. Our Google Ads account reads some of its events to measure our advertising (section 2.3). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — only if you opt in to marketing cookies on the marketing website (section 2.4). Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy; see Meta's Privacy Policy and Meta's Cookies Policy. You can also manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

Apart from the opt-in services above, we do not embed social media widgets, comment systems, video players, or other third-party components that place their own cookies or tracking technologies on your device.

4. How to Manage Your Preferences

Consent Banner and Preferences Centre

On the marketing website, optional cookies are governed by the consent banner. Nothing optional is set by default; the banner offers "Accept all", "Accept essential", and "Deny" with equal prominence, plus per-category choices via "Manage individual choices". You can change or withdraw your choice at any time:

Withdrawing consent takes effect immediately: the page reloads without the withdrawn trackers, and the associated cookies are no longer used by us. (To also remove the cookie files themselves from your device, clear site data for atteste.art in your browser.)

Browser Controls

You can manage or delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Each browser provides different controls:

  • Clearing site data — most browsers allow you to clear cookies, IndexedDB, and localStorage for a specific site through the browser settings or developer tools.
  • Blocking storage — you can configure your browser to block all cookies and local storage, but this will prevent Attesté from functioning.
  • Private/Incognito browsing — using private browsing mode means all local storage is cleared when you close the browser window.

Please note that if you clear your browser's local storage, you will be signed out of Attesté and will need to log in again. Your data is not lost because it is stored securely on our servers.

In-App Controls

You can manage functional preferences directly within the application:

  • Theme preference — go to Settings to toggle between dark and light mode.
  • Sign out — signing out clears your authentication session and cached data.

5. Data Retention

Strictly necessary storage is retained only for as long as needed for its stated purpose. Authentication tokens expire and refresh automatically. Cached data is cleared when you sign out. Functional preferences persist until you change them or clear your browser data.

We do not retain any local storage data beyond what is described in this policy.

6. International Data Transfers

Firebase services process data in the United States. Authentication tokens and cached data stored on your device remain on your device and are not transferred, but the underlying data they represent is synchronised with Firebase servers located in the United States. Appropriate safeguards are in place as described in our Privacy Policy.
