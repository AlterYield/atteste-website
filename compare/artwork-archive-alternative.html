The short answer

Attesté is the closest AI-native alternative to Artwork Archive: a mobile-first art collection app with a built-in AI assistant (Atelier), a genuine free tier with no credit card, and cryptographic provenance you can seal at the moment a work is created (First Witness) — none of which Artwork Archive offers. Where Artwork Archive is the better choice

  • Consignment-heavy galleries: Artwork Archive's consignment workflows are mature; Attesté doesn't offer them yet.
  • You want a public presence: Artwork Archive gives you a public profile, embeddable pages, and a Discovery marketplace with a Call-for-Entry board.
  • Track record matters to you: 15 years in market, used across 130+ countries — trust a new brand can't match yet.
  • Larger teams: up to 10 users with view-only roles and white-glove data import on organisation plans.

Frequently asked questions

Artwork Archive alternative — the questions people actually ask.

Is Artwork Archive free?
No. Artwork Archive offers a 14-day free trial (no credit card required) but has no permanently free plan; paid plans start around $10/month. Attesté has a genuine free tier — Explorer — with no card, so you can start cataloguing for free.
Does Artwork Archive have a mobile app?
Artwork Archive is web-based and does not offer a native mobile app. Attesté is mobile-first on iOS and Android, so you can photograph and catalogue a work right at the wall.
Does Artwork Archive use AI?
No. Artwork Archive has no AI features. Attesté includes Atelier, a voice-and-camera AI assistant that identifies works from a photo, drafts descriptions and follow-ups, and helps document condition and insurance.
How do I switch from Artwork Archive to Attesté?
Export your collection from Artwork Archive as a CSV, then use Attesté's AI-assisted import and document scan-to-fill to bring records in. Photograph any works that need images and Atelier helps fill in the details.
Is Attesté cheaper than Artwork Archive?
For individual collectors, yes. Attesté Premium is $19.99/month for unlimited artworks, versus Artwork Archive's top collector plan at roughly $36–42/month. Attesté also has a free tier; Artwork Archive does not. Feature sets differ — see the comparison above.
