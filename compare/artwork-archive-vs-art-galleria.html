Artwork Archive vs Art Galleria

Two of the best-known art collection tools, compared honestly — pricing, features, apps, and who each is really for. Then the one thing neither of them does: AI, a free tier, and provenance recorded at the moment of creation.

The short answer

Artwork Archive is the better value for individual collectors and artists — cheaper plans (from ~$10/mo), a Discovery marketplace and Call-for-Entry board, and 15 years in market — but it's web-only with no app. Art Galleria is the stronger fit for selling galleries: built-in e-commerce, a website builder, accounting sync, and native apps including Apple TV — at a higher price. Neither has AI, a genuine free tier, or provenance-at-creation — which is where Attesté, a newer AI-native option, differs.

Frequently asked questions

Artwork Archive vs Art Galleria — the questions people actually ask.

What is the main difference between Artwork Archive and Art Galleria?
Artwork Archive is a web-based cataloguing tool that's better value for individual collectors and artists and includes a Discovery marketplace, but has no native mobile app. Art Galleria is a heavier gallery back-office with built-in e-commerce, a website builder, accounting sync and native apps (including Apple TV), aimed at selling galleries — and it costs more.
Which is cheaper, Artwork Archive or Art Galleria?
Artwork Archive is cheaper, especially for collectors — plans start around $10/month and top out near $42, versus Art Galleria's roughly $36 to $180 for collectors. On gallery/organisation plans Artwork Archive also generally undercuts Art Galleria.
Do Artwork Archive or Art Galleria have AI?
No. As of 2026 neither Artwork Archive nor Art Galleria offers AI features. If AI matters to you — identifying works from a photo, drafting descriptions and follow-ups, voice assistance — Attesté is the AI-native option in this category.
Which has a mobile app, Artwork Archive or Art Galleria?
Art Galleria has native iOS and Android apps (plus Apple TV and AR wall preview). Artwork Archive is web-only with no native app. Attesté is also mobile-first on iOS and Android.
Is there a free alternative to Artwork Archive and Art Galleria?
Yes. Neither Artwork Archive nor Art Galleria has a permanently free plan (both use trials). Attesté offers a genuine free tier — Explorer — with no credit card, plus a built-in AI assistant and provenance recorded at creation.
