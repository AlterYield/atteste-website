The AI-native alternative to Art Galleria

Art Galleria is a capable, mature gallery back-office — e-commerce, websites, accounting sync and native apps. Attesté competes on a different axis: a built-in AI assistant, a genuine free tier, provenance recorded at creation, and collector pricing a fraction of Art Galleria's. Here's an honest side-by-side, including where Art Galleria still wins.

The short answer

Attesté is an AI-native alternative to Art Galleria: a built-in AI assistant (Atelier), an AI gallery concierge (Almanac), a genuine free tier with no card, and cryptographic provenance sealed at the moment of creation (First Witness) — none of which Art Galleria offers. Art Galleria remains stronger for built-in e-commerce and payments, its website builder and CMS sync, accounting integrations (Xero/QuickBooks), and consignment. For individual collectors, Attesté is far cheaper — unlimited artworks at $19.99/month vs Art Galleria's ~$180.

Where Art Galleria is the better choice

  • You sell online: Art Galleria has built-in e-commerce (Stripe, Apple/Google Pay) and invoicing; Attesté never processes payments by design.
  • You want a website: Art Galleria includes a website builder and syncs to WordPress, Shopify, Wix and Squarespace.
  • Accounting + integrations: Xero auto-sync, QuickBooks export, and a public API/webhooks — Attesté has none of these yet.
  • Consignment-driven galleries: in/out consignment, due-backs and reports are mature in Art Galleria.
  • Established operation: in market since 2013, six currencies, included setup and 1:1 onboarding on all plans.

Frequently asked questions

Art Galleria alternative — the questions people actually ask.

Is Art Galleria free?
No. Art Galleria offers a 14-day free trial that requires a credit card, and has no permanently free plan. Attesté has a genuine free tier — Explorer — with no card required.
Does Art Galleria have AI?
No. Art Galleria has no AI features. Attesté includes Atelier, a voice-and-camera AI assistant, plus Almanac, an AI gallery concierge that drafts exhibition copy, artist bios, artwork descriptions and collector follow-ups.
Is Attesté cheaper than Art Galleria?
For collectors, dramatically. Attesté Premium is $19.99/month for unlimited artworks versus Art Galleria's unlimited collector plan at roughly $150–180/month. Attesté's gallery entry plan ($64.99/month) is also lower than Art Galleria's starter ($65–78/month), though feature sets differ.
Does Attesté do e-commerce and websites like Art Galleria?
No. Art Galleria is stronger here — it offers built-in e-commerce (Stripe, Apple/Google Pay), a website builder with CMS sync, and accounting integrations. Attesté deliberately never processes payments; it focuses on cataloguing, AI, and provenance.
Does Art Galleria have a mobile app?
Yes — Art Galleria has iOS and Android apps plus an Apple TV app and AR wall preview. Attesté is also mobile-first on iOS and Android; the difference is Attesté's built-in AI and consumer "Strava for Art" experience, not app availability.
