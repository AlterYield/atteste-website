How Artists Use Attesté

A simple flow from catalogue to certificate to collector relationship.

1

Set Up Your Profile

Public artist page with bio, statement, links, and selected works. The page lives at attesté.art and is indexable on the gallery directory.

2

Catalogue Your Works

Upload images, dimensions, medium, year, edition, and your own narrative. Bulk import from CSV or add one piece at a time.

3

Issue Source Certificates

When you sell, generate a tamper-evident certificate of attestation. Each one carries a public QR-verified record that proves the piece came from you.

4

Stay in the Loop

When a collector logs an encounter or asks to verify a piece they bought from you, you get a notification. Approve, decline, or add provenance.
