Certificate of authenticity for a painting

A painting's certificate of authenticity should name the medium and the support separately, give the unframed size (height first), say where the work is signed and what is on the reverse, and carry a clear photograph of the whole painting. What details does a painting COA need?

  • Medium and support, separately. "Oil on Belgian linen", "acrylic on gesso panel", "watercolour on 300 gsm cotton paper". The support matters for conservation and insurance.
  • Size, unframed, height × width. Add depth for deep stretchers or cradled panels. If you sell it framed, give the framed size as a second measurement and say so.
  • Edition: "Unique". A painting is one of one; say it plainly so nobody wonders about copies.
  • Year completed. If the painting was worked on over years, write the span, e.g. "2023–2025".

Where should I describe the signature and the reverse?

Use the statement field. Say where you signed (for example "signed lower right recto") and record everything on the back: inscriptions, the title as written, stretcher stamps, gallery or exhibition labels. Collectors and later owners compare these details with the painting itself, so be exact.

Should I mention varnish, retouching or framing?

Yes, briefly. Note whether the painting is varnished, with what and when, or whether it is left unvarnished on purpose. Mention any later work you did on it, such as retouching or restretching. If you chose the frame, say so. A conservator decades from now will thank you.

How should I photograph a painting for its certificate?

Shoot the whole painting square-on, out of its frame if you can, in even, glare-free light, with colours as close to the real thing as you can manage. Crop to the edge of the canvas. Then save that file and never edit it: the fingerprint on the certificate matches only that exact file. Keep detail shots of the signature and the reverse alongside it.

Do I need one certificate per painting?

Yes. One certificate describes one work. For a diptych or a series sold together, list every panel in the statement and give the total size, or issue one certificate per panel if they could ever be sold apart.
