No commission on sales, no per-scan charges, no setup fees — and you can cancel anytime.

How gallery subscriptions work today

Gallery subscriptions are activated with an activation code issued by our team after you sign up. Email galleries@atteste.art to get started; we'll set up your gallery and send your code.

Redeeming your activation code

  1. Open your gallery in the Attesté app and go to the activation screen (the link in your activation email takes you straight there).
  2. Enter the 12-character code from your email. Don't worry about capitalisation or dashes — the field handles both.
  3. Tap Activate Subscription. You'll see a confirmation with your access period (typically 12 months) and the date your subscription runs until.

If the code is rejected:

  • "Invalid code" — check for typos against the email; codes are 12 characters.
  • "Already used" — each code can be redeemed once. If you believe this is an error, contact us.
  • "Expired" — codes have a redemption window; email us for a fresh one.

Activation help: galleries@atteste.art.
