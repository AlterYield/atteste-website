Attesté for artists

Attesté lets artists issue source certificates — certificates of attestation created at the source, by you, when a work leaves your studio. A source certificate travels with the work, so collectors and galleries can verify authenticity and provenance from day one.

How do I subscribe?

Artist plan signup is rolling out. To get set up today, email info@atteste.art with a link to your practice (website or Instagram) and we'll get you started. If a gallery represents you and has an Attesté Professional or Enterprise subscription, ask them to provision your Represented access — there's no cost to you.
