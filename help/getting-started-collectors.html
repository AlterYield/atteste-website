Getting started as a collector

What is Attesté?

Attesté is an art collection companion for collectors: it keeps a living record of the artworks you own and the art you encounter. You can log gallery visits into a personal Art Journey timeline, hold certificates of attestation for your artworks, track condition over time, and get AI-assisted recommendations for what to see next.

The app is free to download on the App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android), with optional paid subscriptions. A web version exists, but subscriptions can only be purchased on iOS or Android.

What's free, forever

The Explorer tier is free and includes:

  • Up to 25 artworks in your collection
  • Basic collection management
  • Your Art Journey timeline of gallery visits
  • Walk discovery trails through local galleries
  • 5 AI-assisted actions per month

Your first ten minutes

  1. Download and sign in. You'll be asked to accept the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, age confirmation, and Community Guidelines once.
  2. Add your first artwork. Photograph it and fill in what you know — details can always be edited later.
  3. Log a gallery visit. Check in when you visit a gallery and it joins your Art Journey timeline and map.
  4. Try a discovery trail. Trails guide you through galleries near you.
