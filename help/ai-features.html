AI features

Our AI honesty rules: AI-generated content is labelled. AI price commentary is experimental, collapsed by default, and never counted in your portfolio totals. Provenance records are never AI-invented. If the AI doesn't know, it says so. Full detail in our AI Disclosure.

The Companion (voice)

The Companion is a real-time voice assistant you can talk to about your collection and the art around you. It can answer questions about your artworks, guide you through the app on a spoken tour, and coach a Talking Provenance recording. It uses read-only access to your collection — it can look things up and navigate, but it doesn't change your data.

Voice conversations ask for consent on first use. Audio is streamed to Google's Gemini Live service in real time, is not stored after the session, and is never used for voice identification or to train models. There's also a text-based AI chat if you'd rather type.

AI Sommelier

The Sommelier builds a taste profile from your collection and activity, then recommends exhibitions and discoveries matched to it (available from the Collector tier). It works from your artwork metadata and notes — it never receives your photos, financial data, or personal messages.

Condition analysis

The condition analyzer guides you through multi-angle photos of a work and drafts an AI condition assessment you review and edit before saving — building a condition history over time. Included with the Condition Vault from the Collector tier; condition reports can be exported for insurance purposes.

Year in Art

An annual, narrated review of your collecting year, generated from your own activity.

AI usage limits

  • Explorer (free): 5 AI actions per month
  • Collector: 50 AI actions per month
  • Premium / Patron: unlimited

Limits reset monthly. If you hit a limit, features that don't use AI keep working normally.

Questions or something looks wrong?

If an AI answer looks off, you can flag it in-app or email info@atteste.art — AI-content reviews go to the same address.
