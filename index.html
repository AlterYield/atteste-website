The Quiet Crisis

Art Stories Are Dying With Their Owners

Every art collection holds decades of stories — the gallery where you first saw it, the artist who signed it, the trip that changed your taste. But without a system to capture these moments, they vanish. Receipts fade. Memories blur. Heirs inherit art they know nothing about.

Insurance valuations expire in drawers. Provenance lives on Post-it notes. And the personal journey — the most valuable part of any collection — is never recorded at all.

The Provenance Promise

Bank-Grade Integrity for Art

Every record in Attesté is secured with SHA-256 cryptographic hashing — the same standard used in financial transactions. Our provenance chain is append-only: records can be added but never altered or deleted.

This isn't blockchain hype. It's the quiet certainty that your collection's story — every acquisition, every valuation, every transfer — is sealed in cryptographic truth. Permanent. Verifiable. Yours.

And our valuation philosophy is simple: invoice is king. AI provides experimental insight, but your portfolio totals only reflect documented, professional valuations. We believe trust is earned through honesty, not inflated numbers.

Built in South Africa

A father's project became his daughters' business

Attesté started when a private collector realised his children would inherit his art — but not the stories behind it. What began as a weekend project to digitise one collection grew into a platform that could serve every collector.

Today, Attesté is a family enterprise: built by a father, operated by three daughters, and powered by the belief that every art collection tells a story worth preserving.
