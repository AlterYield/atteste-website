From studio to wall, and onwards

One place for the whole life of a work of art.

Prove.
A certificate anyone can check

Every work gets an Attesté certificate, signed when it’s issued and timestamped on Bitcoin. Share the link or the QR code; whoever opens it sees whether the record still matches, with no account and no app.

Own.
The paperwork, finally in one place

Invoices, condition notes and valuations live with the work they belong to. When the insurer asks, you export one tidy PDF instead of an afternoon in the filing cabinet.

Show.
Out in the world

Artists get a public page at atteste.art/artist/their-name, with their works and certificates on it. Galleries get a place in the gallery directory inside the app, where collectors browse before a Saturday of gallery-hopping.

Pass it on.
The record travels with the work

Sold, gifted or consigned, the work’s record moves to its next owner, and every hand-over is signed and added to the chain.

Sell.
No more wire-and-pray

Sell inside Attesté. The buyer pays a licensed escrow service, not a stranger’s bank account, and the money is released once the buyer confirms the work arrived. Ownership and the certificate move to the buyer at the same time.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked

What is Attesté?

Attesté is an AI-powered art collection management platform. It helps collectors catalogue their art, verify provenance with SHA-256 cryptographic certificates, discover new galleries and artists via GPS-gated trails, and document collection history across generations.

Can my AI assistant check an Attesté certificate?

Yes. Attesté runs a public, read-only connector at https://mcp.atteste.art/mcp that any assistant supporting the Model Context Protocol (MCP) can use. Paste a certificate link and the assistant can check the signature, fetch the Bitcoin timestamp proof, read the ownership history as roles and dates, and explain what the certificate proves and doesn’t. It can’t see your collection, change anything, or buy or sell. Setup guide.

How much does Attesté cost?

Attesté has a free Explorer tier (25 artworks, 5 AI analyses/month). Paid collector tiers start at $9.99/month (Collector — 200 artworks, full AI Sommelier) and $19.99/month (Premium — unlimited, Legacy Vault), with 17% savings on annual plans. Artist plans start at $14.99/month (Studio, 14-day free trial). Gallery plans are monthly subscriptions sold via the web: Boutique $64.99/mo, Professional $158.99/mo, Enterprise $259.00/mo.

Is my art collection data private?

Yes. By default every collection is private. Public profiles require an explicit opt-in. Data is hosted in Firebase's Johannesburg data centre (africa-south1), so personal data stays in Africa by default. Attesté is POPIA and GDPR aware.

How does AI Art Identification work?

You photograph an artwork with the Attesté app. Google's latest Gemini vision models analyse the image and return likely artist, medium, period and similar works, with an explicit confidence score. Low-confidence guesses are flagged for human verification.

What is an Attesté Certificate?

A SHA-256 cryptographically-secured provenance certificate. It includes the owner, acquisition date and source, a photograph fingerprint, condition and valuation history, and the chain of custody. The hash is published to an immutable log so tampering is detectable.

Does Attesté authenticate whether an artwork is real?

No. Attesté generates a tamper-evident record of what the owner reported. Authentication is a gallery, auction house or expert's job. You can upload professional authentication documents and attach them to the certificate.

Can Attesté help me document an inherited art collection?

Yes. Attesté's AI Art Scout is especially useful for inheritors: photograph each piece and the app identifies likely artist, medium and period. Upgrade to Premium to unlock the Legacy Vault for preserving stories, artist interviews, and acquisition context across generations.

How do galleries use Attesté?

Galleries join on a monthly subscription sold via the web — Boutique ($64.99/mo), Professional ($158.99/mo), or Enterprise ($259.00/mo). They get inventory and exhibition tools, an aggregate collector analytics dashboard, Talking Provenance Curator's Commentary (Professional+), AI-generated exhibition posts, a daily Pulse briefing, the Almanac AI concierge, and a measurable collector pipeline. Analytics are aggregate-only — individual collector data is never shared with galleries.

Where is Attesté available?

The Attesté app works worldwide. POPIA compliance (South Africa) is complete; GDPR compliance (EU) is in progress; US and UK markets are supported. Payment methods vary by region. Contact info@atteste.art for your specific market.
