## Scarcity, Social Proof, and Urgency Mechanics

### FOMO Framework

Art collectors are driven by six psychological forces. Each Attesté FOMO tactic maps to one or more of these:

| Force | Definition | Collector Translation |
|---|---|---|
| **Loss aversion** | People feel losses more strongly than gains | "What will I lose if I don't act?" |
| **Social proof** | People follow the behaviour of peers they respect | "Other collectors like me are doing this" |
| **Scarcity** | Limited availability increases perceived value | "This won't be available forever" |
| **Urgency** | Time pressure accelerates decisions | "Act now or miss the window" |
| **Status** | People seek recognition and position within groups | "This elevates me as a collector" |
| **Fear of irreversibility** | People fear making mistakes they can't undo | "If I lose documentation now, I can never recover it" |

## Tactic 2: The Loss Calculator

**Type:** Loss Aversion + Fear of Irreversibility
**Format:** Interactive tool on website + social content
**Target:** C4 David, C5 Catherine, C6 Richard

### Mechanics

An interactive calculator on the Attesté website (and simplified versions in social content) that quantifies what a collector stands to lose:

**Input fields:**
1. How many works do you own? (slider: 1–500+)
2. Estimated total value? (slider: R10K–R10M+)
3. Are they insured? (Yes/No/Partially)
4. Do you have certificates of authenticity for all works? (Yes/No/Some)
5. Do you have a succession plan for your art? (Yes/No)

**Output:**
A personalized "risk report" showing:
- **Estimated value at risk** (uninsured works)
- **Potential resale loss** from missing provenance/COAs (calculated as 20–40% of value)
- **Estate risk score** (based on succession plan status)
- **One-line recommendation:** "You should protect your collection. Start free with Attesté."

### Social Content Versions

**Instagram carousel: "What would you lose?"**
- Slide 1: "You have 30 paintings. How many are insured?"
- Slide 2: "Average SA collector is underinsured by 35%"
- Slide 3: "That painting you bought without a receipt? It loses 40% of resale value"
- Slide 4: "Fire. Theft. Flood. Divorce. It happens."
- Slide 5: "Calculate your risk → [link in bio]"

**Twitter/X thread:**
> Your collection is worth R2M.
>
> You have no insurance. No certificates for 8 works. No succession plan.
>
> Here's what you actually stand to lose:
> - R2M in a total loss event (fire, theft)
> - R400K in resale value from missing provenance
> - 6 months of family conflict over who gets what
>
> Total risk: R2.4M + your family relationships.
>
> Or: R9/month. →

## Tactic 3: Social Proof Engine

**Type:** Social Proof + Status
**Format:** Ongoing, cross-channel
**Target:** All personas

### Mechanics

A systematic programme to generate and distribute social proof at every stage of the funnel.

**Level 1: Numbers (Awareness stage)**

| Proof Point | Trigger | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| "500 collectors joined in launch week" | Weekly milestone | All |
| "10,000 works catalogued on Attesté" | Work count milestone | All |
| "R50M in art documented on Attesté" | Value milestone | All |
| "Collectors in 12 countries" | Geographic milestone | All |

**Level 2: Peer Stories (Consideration stage)**

| Proof Type | Format | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Collector Spotlight** | Instagram carousel + Reel, blog post | Weekly |
| **Gallery Case Study** | LinkedIn article, blog post, email | Monthly |
| **Advisor Testimonial** | LinkedIn post, email, sales collateral | Monthly |
| **"Before/After"** | Instagram carousel: phone gallery mess → Attesté beauty | Bi-weekly |

**Level 3: Specific Outcomes (Decision stage)**

| Outcome | Proof | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| "I discovered I was underinsured by R8M" | Richard's story | Email, ads, landing page |
| "I sold a work for R8K more because I had proper documentation" | Thabo's story | Ads, blog, email |
| "Art fair prep went from 3 weeks to 2 days" | Sarah's story | B2B sales, LinkedIn |
| "Annual reports now take 5 hours, not 25" | Michael's story | B2B sales, LinkedIn |
| "Our audit went from 3 weeks to 5 days" | Nomsa's story | Enterprise sales |

### Social Proof Distribution Rules
1. **Specificity beats generality:** "Saved R8M in insurance gap" > "Saved money"
2. **Peer relevance:** Show C7 Zanele a collector like her, not C6 Richard
3. **Recency:** Update proof points monthly. Stale testimonials lose credibility
4. **Consent always:** Every story, number, and quote requires explicit user permission

## Tactic 4: Urgency Triggers

**Type:** Urgency + Loss Aversion
**Format:** Email, in-app, social
**Target:** Varies by trigger

### Calendar-Based Urgency

| Trigger Event | Timing | Message | Target Persona |
|---|---|---|---|
| Art fair approaching | 4 weeks before | "The fair is in 4 weeks. Is your collection documented?" | C2, C7, C8 |
| Insurance renewal season | October–November (SA annual cycle) | "Your insurance renewal is coming. Is your art register current?" | C4, C5, C6 |
| Tax year end | February (SA) | "Need your art asset schedule for tax? Export from Attesté in one click" | C5, C8, B2 |
| Holiday season | November–December | "Give the gift of a documented collection" | C4, C6 |
| Year end | December | "Your collection grew this year. Did your documentation?" | All |
| New Year | January | "New year, new collection goals. Start documenting" | C1, C2, C3 |

### Behaviour-Based Urgency (In-App + Email)

| User Behaviour | Trigger | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Free user hits 20 of 25 work limit | In-app notification | "You're almost at your free limit. Upgrade to keep cataloguing" |
| User hasn't logged in for 14 days | Email | "Your collection is waiting. 3 new features since your last visit" |
| User added works but no insurance export | In-app prompt at Day 30 | "You've catalogued 45 works worth R800K. They're not insured. Export your Insurance Vault now" |
| User catalogued 50+ works with no provenance | In-app prompt | "23 works in your collection have no provenance documentation. Here's how to fix that" |
| User on Standard for 3+ months with high usage | Email | "You're using Attesté like a Premium collector. Here's what you're missing" |

### External Urgency (News-Jacking)

When real-world events create natural urgency, Attesté responds quickly:

| Event Type | Response | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Major art theft reported | "Is your collection documented well enough for a police report?" | Twitter/X, Instagram Stories |
| Natural disaster / fire | "Collectors affected by [event]: your documentation could be your lifeline" | Email, Twitter/X |
| Celebrity art dispute / divorce | "Art disputes happen. Provenance and documentation prevent them" | Twitter/X thread |
| Record auction result for SA artist | "This artist just sold for R2M. Is your work by them properly valued?" | Twitter/X, Instagram |
| Gallery closure / artist estate dispute | "When galleries close, provenance becomes your only proof of purchase" | Blog, Twitter/X |

**Rules for news-jacking:**
- Never exploit tragedy directly. Always frame as education, not opportunism
- Respond within 24 hours of the event (speed matters)
- Always offer genuine value (a guide, a tip, a free feature) — not just a sales pitch
- Tone: concerned expert, not ambulance chaser

## Tactic 6: The "What If" Series

**Type:** Loss Aversion + Fear of Irreversibility
**Format:** Content series across all channels
**Target:** C4, C5, C6, C7

### Concept

A recurring content series that asks "What if?" questions to make the abstract risk of undocumented art feel concrete and personal.

**Content pieces:**

| "What If..." | Emotional Impact | Attesté Solution |
|---|---|---|
| "What if there's a fire tonight? Can you list every work you'd lose?" | Fear → urgency | Insurance Vault |
| "What if you're in an accident and can't speak? Does anyone know what your collection is worth?" | Vulnerability → action | Documentation + Legacy Vault |
| "What if you try to sell a painting and the buyer asks for proof of purchase?" | Embarrassment → motivation | Provenance Chain |
| "What if your children inherit your collection but don't know any of the stories?" | Sadness → purpose | Collection Memoirs |
| "What if a gallery closes and they were your only proof of purchase?" | Anxiety → preparation | Personal provenance records |
| "What if your home is broken into and you can't describe the stolen works to police?" | Helplessness → action | Documented inventory with images |
| "What if your spouse asks for a fair division of the art in a divorce?" | Dread → practicality | Comprehensive valuation records |

**Format variations:**
- Instagram: single-image posts with the question in bold text over a blurred artwork
- Instagram Stories: interactive poll ("Could you list every work you own right now? Yes / No")
- Twitter/X: standalone tweets, threading into solutions
- Email: "What if" subject lines with story-based content
- Blog: long-form "What If" scenarios with detailed case studies

**Type:** Social Proof + Status
**Format:** In-app, email, social
**Target:** C5, C7, C8, B1, B2

### Mechanics

Show users how they compare to peers — not to shame, but to motivate completion and engagement.

**In-app comparisons (anonymized):**

| Metric | Display | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| "Your collection is 60% documented. Similar collectors average 85%" | Progress bar | Motivates completion |
| "You've added provenance to 12 of 45 works. Collectors your size average 32" | Fraction + benchmark | Provenance completion drive |
| "Your Insurance Vault is ready to export. Only 45% of collectors have done this" | Status + percentile | Insurance activation |
| "You're in the top 20% of active Attesté users" | Percentile badge | Retention + pride |

Subject: "Your collection vs. similar collectors"
Content:

## Tactic 8: Limited Feature Previews

**Type:** Scarcity + Status
**Format:** Email, in-app, social
**Target:** Power users (C5, C7, C8)

### Mechanics

Before launching major new features, offer exclusive early access to a limited number of users.

**Process:**
1. Announce upcoming feature on social: "Something big is coming to Attesté"
2. Email to top 10% most active users: "You've been selected for early access to [Feature]. Only 100 spots."
3. Early access users test and provide feedback for 2 weeks
4. Early access users share their experience (encouraged, not required)
5. Feature launches publicly with early access testimonials as social proof

**Features suited to this tactic:**
- Market Intelligence / Valuation Tools (C8 excitement)
- Collection Memoirs audio recording (C6 emotional hook)
- AI-powered artwork identification (C1, C3 wow factor)
- Gallery Showcase public profiles (C7 sharing drive)
- Advanced analytics dashboard (C8, B2 data hook)

## Tactic 9: The Attesté Guarantee

**Type:** Risk reversal (counters loss aversion about adopting a new tool)
**Format:** Website, email, sales conversations
**Target:** Sceptical personas (C4, C6, B1, B3)

### Mechanics

| Guarantee | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Data portability** | "Your data is yours. Export everything — every image, every record, every document — at any time, in standard formats. If you leave, you take everything with you." |
| **No lock-in** | "Cancel any time. No contracts (except enterprise). No penalties. No dark patterns." |
| **Privacy by default** | "Your collection is private unless you explicitly choose to share. We never sell data. We never show your collection to anyone without your permission." |
| **Uptime commitment** | "Your documentation is available when you need it. 99.9% uptime SLA." |

**Why this is a FOMO tactic:** By removing the risk of adoption, the guarantee shifts the FOMO calculation. The question is no longer "What if this platform doesn't work?" but "What am I losing by NOT using it?"

## FOMO Ethics Charter

Attesté's FOMO marketing must follow these principles:

1. **Real risks only.** Never fabricate or exaggerate threats. Art loss, insurance gaps, and provenance failures are real risks — we don't need to invent them
2. **Always offer value.** Every FOMO message must be paired with a genuine solution, not just anxiety. "You're uninsured" must be followed by "Here's how to fix that"
3. **Never shame.** The tone is "concerned friend," not "judgemental expert." Many collectors don't know what they don't know — educate, don't humiliate
4. **Transparent scarcity.** If we say "500 spots," there are 500 spots. No artificial limits that reset secretly
5. **Respect privacy.** All social proof uses anonymized data or explicit user consent. Never expose collection details without permission
6. **No dark patterns.** Clear cancellation, clear pricing, clear data policies. FOMO drives sign-ups; product quality drives retention
