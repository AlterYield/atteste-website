# Attesté FOMO Marketing Plan — Part 6: Metrics, KPIs & Budget Framework

---

## Measurement Philosophy

Every marketing action must be measurable, attributable, and tied to business outcomes. Vanity metrics (impressions, followers) are tracked for awareness context but never used to justify spend. The north star is **revenue growth driven by collector acquisition and retention.**

---

## 1. Funnel Metrics

### The Attesté Funnel

```
AWARENESS → INTEREST → SIGN-UP → ACTIVATION → CONVERSION → RETENTION → REFERRAL
   |            |          |           |             |            |           |
 Reach      Website    Account    First work    Free→Paid    Monthly     Invite
            visit      created    uploaded      upgrade      active      sent
                                                             usage
```

| Stage | Metric | Target (Month 1) | Target (Month 6) | Target (Month 12) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Social media reach (monthly) | 200K | 1M | 3M |
| **Awareness** | Brand search volume | Baseline | +100% | +300% |
| **Interest** | Website unique visitors (monthly) | 5,000 | 15,000 | 40,000 |
| **Interest** | Website → sign-up page conversion | 20% | 25% | 30% |
| **Sign-up** | Total sign-ups (cumulative) | 500 | 2,500 | 5,000 |
| **Sign-up** | Sign-up page → account created | 40% | 50% | 55% |
| **Activation** | First work uploaded within 24 hours | 60% | 70% | 75% |
| **Activation** | 5+ works uploaded within 7 days | 40% | 50% | 55% |
| **Conversion** | Free → Standard (within 30 days) | 12% | 18% | 22% |
| **Conversion** | Free → Premium (within 30 days) | 3% | 5% | 7% |
| **Conversion** | Standard → Premium (within 90 days) | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| **Retention** | Monthly active users (MAU) | 65% | 70% | 75% |
| **Retention** | Monthly churn (paying users) | <5% | <3% | <2.5% |
| **Referral** | Users who send at least one referral | 15% | 25% | 30% |
| **Referral** | Referral → sign-up conversion | 30% | 35% | 40% |

## 2. Revenue Metrics

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Projections

**Assumptions:**
- Standard: $9/mo (~R160/mo)
- Premium: $19/mo (~R340/mo)
- Gallery: ~R500/mo (estimated average)
- Advisor: ~R800/mo (estimated average, multi-client)
- Enterprise: ~R10,000/mo (estimated average)

| Month | Free Users | Standard | Premium | Gallery | Advisor | Enterprise | Total MRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 400 | 60 | 15 | 5 | 2 | 0 | R16,740 |
| 3 | 1,200 | 180 | 50 | 12 | 5 | 1 | R55,800 |
| 6 | 2,000 | 350 | 100 | 20 | 8 | 2 | R110,400 |
| 9 | 2,800 | 500 | 160 | 28 | 12 | 3 | R168,000 |
| 12 | 3,500 | 700 | 230 | 35 | 15 | 4 | R240,100 |

**Year 1 total revenue projection: ~R1.5M–R2M**

### Revenue KPIs

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **MRR** | Monthly recurring revenue | R240K by Month 12 |
| **ARR** | Annual recurring revenue (MRR × 12) | R2.9M by Month 12 |
| **ARPU** | Average revenue per paying user | R180/mo |
| **LTV** | Lifetime value (ARPU × average lifespan) | R5,400 (30-month avg lifespan) |
| **CAC** | Customer acquisition cost | <R500 (consumer), <R2,000 (B2B) |
| **LTV:CAC ratio** | Lifetime value to acquisition cost | >5:1 (consumer), >8:1 (B2B) |
| **Payback period** | Months to recover CAC | <3 months (consumer), <6 months (B2B) |
| **Net revenue retention** | Revenue from existing customers (including upgrades/downgrades) | >105% |
| **Expansion revenue** | Revenue from upgrades as % of total | 15% |

## 3. Channel Performance Metrics

### Per-Channel KPIs

| Channel | Primary Metric | Secondary Metric | Target CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram (organic)** | Follower → sign-up conversion | Engagement rate | R0 (organic) |
| **Instagram (paid)** | Cost per sign-up | ROAS (return on ad spend) | R80–R150 |
| **Twitter/X (organic)** | Impressions → website clicks | Thread engagement rate | R0 (organic) |
| **Twitter/X (paid)** | Cost per sign-up | Click-through rate | R100–R200 |
| **LinkedIn (organic)** | Post engagement → demo request | Article views | R0 (organic) |
| **LinkedIn (paid)** | Cost per lead (B2B) | Lead → demo conversion | R300–R600 |
| **Google SEM** | Cost per sign-up | Keyword → conversion rate | R150–R300 |
| **Google SEO** | Organic traffic growth | Blog → sign-up conversion | R0 (content cost amortized) |
| **Email** | Open rate / click rate | Sequence → conversion rate | R0 (operational cost) |
| **Referral** | Referral → sign-up rate | Referral invites sent per user | R80 (referral reward cost) |
| **Partnerships** | Partner-attributed sign-ups | Partner → paid conversion rate | R100–R250 |
| **PR** | Media mentions | Referral traffic from coverage | R0 (time cost only) |

### Monthly Channel Review Template

Every month, evaluate each channel against:

1. **Volume:** How many sign-ups did this channel generate?
2. **Quality:** What % of sign-ups from this channel activated? Converted to paid? Retained at Month 3?
3. **Cost:** What was the CAC for this channel?
4. **Trend:** Is performance improving, stable, or declining?
5. **Action:** Increase budget, maintain, decrease, or kill?

## 6. Reporting Framework

### Daily Dashboard (Founder/CEO View)

| Metric | Format |
|---|---|
| New sign-ups (today) | Number + trend arrow |
| Active users (today) | Number |
| New paid conversions (today) | Number + revenue |
| MRR | Running total |
| Churn events (today) | Number + alert if above threshold |
| Support tickets | Number + sentiment |

### Weekly Marketing Review

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Sign-up velocity | This week vs. last week vs. target |
| Channel performance | Sign-ups, CAC, quality by channel |

| Email performance | Open rates, click rates, sequence conversions |
| Partnership pipeline | New partners, partner-attributed sign-ups |

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| MRR and growth | Revenue, growth rate, trajectory vs. plan |
| User cohort analysis | Retention by month of sign-up |
| Persona mix | Sign-ups by persona, conversion by persona |

| Competitive landscape | New entrants, feature parity, pricing changes |

## 7. Budget Framework (Year 1)

### Total Marketing Budget

**Recommended:** 30–40% of ARR (typical for early-stage SaaS)
**Estimated Year 1 ARR:** R2M–R2.9M
**Year 1 Marketing Budget:** R600K–R1.1M

### Budget Breakdown

| Category | % of Budget | Year 1 Estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paid digital (Instagram, Google, Twitter/X, LinkedIn)** | 40% | R240K–R440K | Ramp up monthly as channel ROI becomes clear |
| **Content creation** | 15% | R90K–R165K | Photography, video, design, copywriting |
| **Events & partnerships** | 15% | R90K–R165K | Art fair sponsorships, collector events, partner incentives |
| **Email & marketing tools** | 10% | R60K–R110K | Email platform, analytics, CRM, social scheduling |
| **PR & communications** | 8% | R48K–R88K | Agency retainer or freelance publicist |
| **Referral programme** | 7% | R42K–R77K | Referral rewards (free months, discounts) |
| **Contingency** | 5% | R30K–R55K | Opportunistic spend, testing new channels |

### Monthly Budget Ramp

| Month | Total Spend | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (Launch) | 15% of annual | Heavy awareness spend, PR blitz, launch events |
| 2 | 10% | Conversion optimization, email sequence refinement |
| 3 | 8% | Partnership activation, content marketing ramp |
| 4 | 8% | Art fair spend (if aligned with calendar) |
| 5 | 7% | B2B campaign push |
| 6 | 7% | Mid-year review, rebalance channels |
| 7 | 7% | Discovery content, new user acquisition |
| 8 | 8% | B2B intensification, enterprise pipeline |
| 9 | 7% | Community building, referral push |
| 10 | 8% | Valuation/analytics features marketing, Premium push |
| 11 | 8% | Holiday season, gifting, year-end corporate |
| 12 | 7% | Year-end review, renewal push, reactivation |

### Budget Governance Rules

1. **No channel gets more than 50% of paid spend** — diversification reduces risk
2. **Kill underperformers fast** — if a channel delivers CAC >2x target for 2 consecutive months, cut it
3. **Double down on winners** — if a channel delivers CAC <0.5x target, increase budget by 25% next month
4. **Reserve 5% for experiments** — test one new channel or tactic per quarter
5. **Review allocation monthly** — market conditions and channel performance change constantly

## 8. Success Criteria

### Year 1 Definition of Success

| Criterion | Target | Status Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | R2M+ ARR by Month 12 | Monthly |
| **Users** | 5,000 total sign-ups, 1,000+ paying | Monthly |
| **Retention** | <3% monthly churn (paying) by Month 12 | Monthly |
| **Unit economics** | LTV:CAC > 5:1 (consumer), > 8:1 (B2B) | Quarterly |
| **Brand** | Recognized as the default art collection platform in SA | Qualitative (survey, press coverage) |
| **Product-market fit** | NPS > 50 (consumer), > 60 (B2B) | Quarterly survey |
| **Growth engine** | 30%+ of new sign-ups from referral/organic | Monthly |
| **B2B traction** | 35+ galleries, 15+ advisors, 4+ enterprise contracts | Monthly |
| **Market position** | No direct competitor in SA with >500 users | Quarterly competitive review |

### If We Hit These Numbers...

Year 1 success sets up Year 2 for:
- International expansion (start with UK/African diaspora markets)
- Series A fundraising (if venture-backed) on strong unit economics
- Feature expansion (marketplace, artist profiles, secondary market tools)
- Team scaling (dedicated marketing, sales, and customer success)

### If We Don't...

Triggers for strategic review:
- MRR < R100K at Month 12 → re-evaluate product-market fit
- Monthly churn > 5% at Month 6 → re-evaluate product value proposition
- CAC > 2x target across all channels → re-evaluate marketing strategy
- B2B pipeline < 10 prospects at Month 6 → re-evaluate B2B approach
- NPS < 30 at any point → stop marketing, fix product
