## Demographic Distribution by Market Priority

### Primary Markets (Launch)
- **South Africa** — Home market, strong gallery scene (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Stellenbosch)
- **United Kingdom** — Large collector base, auction culture (London, Edinburgh)
- **United States** — Massive market, art fair culture (NYC, Miami, LA)

### Secondary Markets (6–12 months)
- **Australia** — Growing contemporary scene (Melbourne, Sydney)
- **Netherlands / Germany** — Deep art heritage, fair culture (Amsterdam, Berlin, Cologne)
- **UAE** — Emerging collector wealth (Dubai, Abu Dhabi)

### Tertiary Markets (12–24 months)
- **France, Italy** — Traditional markets, slower digital adoption
- **Japan, South Korea** — Tech-forward collectors, growing contemporary interest
- **Nigeria, Kenya** — Emerging African contemporary art scenes
