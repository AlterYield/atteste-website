# Attesté FOMO Marketing Plan — Part 3: Content Templates

---

## Persona-Specific Messaging Matrix

### How to Use This Document

Every piece of Attesté marketing should be filterable by persona. This matrix provides the **exact language**, **emotional triggers**, and **content angles** that resonate with each persona — so that any team member, freelancer, or agency can create on-brand, on-target content without guessing.

## Master Messaging Matrix

### C1: Mia (The Accidental Collector)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core emotion** | Surprise ("I'm a collector?") → Delight ("This is fun!") → Pride ("Look what I've built") |
| **Voice/tone** | Warm, encouraging, non-intimidating. Never use jargon. Make her feel welcome, not tested |
| **Key words** | "Your art," "beautiful," "easy," "start," "free," "first step," "discover" |
| **Words to avoid** | "Portfolio," "investment," "provenance" (initially), "serious collector," "asset" |
| **Primary pain** | Didn't know she needed this. Photos scattered across devices. No system at all |
| **Primary desire** | A beautiful, simple way to see and share what she has |
| **CTA style** | Soft, curiosity-driven: "See your collection come to life" / "Try it free" |
| **FOMO trigger** | "Other collectors with 10 works started here. Now they have 50+" |

**Sample ad copy (Instagram):**
> You have art on your walls, prints you love, that piece from the market in Kalk Bay. But where are the photos? Where are the details?
>
> Attesté turns your scattered art into a beautiful collection — in minutes.
>
> Start free. No credit card.

**Sample email subject lines:**
- "You might already be an art collector"
- "Your walls tell a story. We help you write it"
- "The easiest way to organize the art you love"

## Cross-Persona FOMO Statistics Bank

Use these across all channels, tailored to persona context:

| Statistic | Source/Basis | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| "67% of private collectors have no insurance on their art" | Industry estimate (adjustable with real data) | C4, C5, C6, C7 |
| "The average collector underestimates their collection's value by 35%" | Valuation comparison data | C5, C8, B2 |
| "1 in 4 works sold without a certificate of authenticity loses 40%+ of resale value" | Art market research | C2, C7, C8 |
| "Only 12% of SA collectors have a formal succession plan for their art" | Survey data needed | C5, C6 |
| "Gallery directors spend 30% of their time on administration" | Industry benchmark | B1 |
| "Corporate collections in SA hold R2B+ in art assets — 70% managed in spreadsheets" | Market sizing estimate | B3 |
| "Art theft in SA increased 23% in the last 3 years" | Crime statistics (verify) | C4, C5, C6 |
| "Collectors who document from first purchase have 3x higher resale returns" | Platform data (build over time) | C2, C8 |

**Important:** Mark statistics that are estimates vs. verified. Build toward verified platform data as the user base grows. Never fabricate statistics — use "industry estimates suggest" when data is approximate.
