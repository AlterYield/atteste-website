## C1: Mia — The Art-Curious Explorer

### Identity Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Mia Chen |
| **Age** | 26 |
| **Location** | Cape Town, South Africa (originally from Taipei) |
| **Occupation** | UX Designer at a fintech startup |
| **Income** | R480,000/year (~$27K USD) |
| **Education** | BA Visual Communication, University of Cape Town |
| **Relationship** | Dating, lives with flatmate in Woodstock |
| **Art Background** | Grew up visiting museums with her mother; took an elective in Art History at UCT |
| **Collection** | 1 linocut print from a First Thursdays market, 2 framed poster prints |
| **Attesté Tier** | Explorer (Free) |

### Psychographic Profile

**Values:** Authenticity, self-expression, community, sustainability
**Personality:** Curious, social, visually driven, slightly impulsive
**Self-image:** "Creative professional who appreciates beautiful things"
**Art identity:** Doesn't call herself a "collector" — feels that word is for wealthy older people

### A Day in Mia's Life

- **7:30 AM** — Scrolls Instagram and TikTok over coffee. Saves posts from galleries and artists she follows
- **9:00 AM** — At work, designing interfaces. Surrounded by design-conscious peers
- **12:30 PM** — Lunch break browsing Etsy/local artist shops for birthday gift ideas
- **6:00 PM** — Meets friends for drinks at a gallery opening in Woodstock. Takes photos of art she likes
- **9:00 PM** — Posts Instagram stories of the opening, tags the gallery
- **10:00 PM** — Lying in bed, googling the artist she liked. Wonders what their work costs

### Current Art Behaviour

**Discovery channels:**
- Instagram (primary — follows 40+ artist/gallery accounts)
- TikTok art content (algorithm-driven discovery)
- First Thursdays in Cape Town (monthly gallery walk)
- Friends' recommendations
- Design blogs and Pinterest

**Barriers to buying:**
- "I don't know enough about art to buy the right thing"
- "What if I'm overpaying?"
- "I can't afford to make a mistake"
- "Galleries feel intimidating — like everyone knows something I don't"
- "I don't have the space for big works"

**Current tools:**
- Instagram saves (her "collection")
- Notes app with artist names
- Google Maps saved pins for galleries
- Screenshot folder on phone (chaotic, never organized)

### The FOMO Triggers

| Trigger | Emotion | Attesté Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sees friends at gallery opening on IG | "I'm missing out on culture" | **Discovery Trails** — curated routes she can follow anytime |
| Artist she follows sells out a drop | "I should have bought when I could" | **AI Art Scout** — alerts for artists matching her taste |
| Can't remember the name of a work she loved | Frustration, regret | **Encounter Log** — snap, save, revisit |
| Feels intimidated at a gallery | Imposter syndrome | **Taste Profile** — "You know more than you think" validation |
| Sees a "Year in Art" post from another user | "I want that cool wrap-up too" | **Year in Art** — but you need to log encounters first |

### Mia's Attesté Journey

**Week 1 — Activation**
- Downloads app after seeing a friend's Discovery Trail on Instagram Stories
- Completes onboarding: selects visual preferences (swipe-based taste quiz)
- Gets her first Taste Profile badge: "Drawn to the Abstract — You gravitate toward texture and emotion over representation"
- Explores her first Discovery Trail: "Woodstock Art Walk — 7 Stops"
- Logs 3 encounters during a Saturday gallery walk
- **Key moment:** "Oh, this is like Strava but for art — I love it"

**Month 1 — Engagement**
- Has logged 18 encounters across 4 gallery visits
- Earned 450 XP, unlocked "Gallery Hopper" badge
- AI Art Scout sends her first recommendation: an emerging printmaker she'd never heard of
- Shares her Taste Profile to Instagram stories — friends comment "this is so you"
- Starts following 3 other users whose trails look interesting
- **Key moment:** Realizes she has a visual diary of her art experiences

**Month 3 — Conversion Consideration**
- Wants to save a work to her "Wishlist" — some features require Collector tier
- AI Sommelier suggests: "Based on your encounters, you'd love these 3 affordable works under R5,000"
- She buys her first "real" artwork: a small mixed-media piece at a First Thursdays pop-up
- Considers upgrading to Collector ($9/mo) to get the Attesté Certificate for her purchase
- **Key moment:** "I just bought my first artwork and I feel like a real collector now"

**Month 6 — Loyalty**
- Upgraded to Collector tier
- Collection: 2 original works + her print, all with Attesté Certificates
- Year in Art preview shows: 47 encounters, 12 galleries, 3 cities, favourite genre shift
- Has become the "art friend" in her group — people ask her for gallery recommendations
- **Key moment:** "I went from 'I don't know anything about art' to 'Let me show you this incredible artist I discovered'"

## C2: James — The Weekend Gallery Hopper

### Identity Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | James Whitfield |
| **Age** | 34 |
| **Location** | East London, UK (Hackney) |
| **Occupation** | Senior Product Manager at a SaaS company |
| **Income** | £62,000/year (~$78K USD) |
| **Education** | MSc Computer Science, Imperial College London |
| **Relationship** | Married to Elena (architect), no kids yet |
| **Art Background** | No formal training; discovered contemporary art through Elena |
| **Collection** | 7 pieces: 2 limited edition prints, 1 original painting, 2 photographs, 2 ceramics |
| **Attesté Tier** | Explorer (Free) → Collector ($9/mo) within 3 months |

### Psychographic Profile

**Values:** Experiences over possessions, intellectual stimulation, taste curation
**Personality:** Analytical, competitive (quietly), data-curious, socially aware
**Self-image:** "Well-rounded professional with interesting hobbies"
**Art identity:** "Elena and I collect together — it's our thing as a couple"

### A Day in James's Life (Saturday)

- **8:30 AM** — Coffee and the Guardian. Checks "This week's exhibitions" section
- **10:00 AM** — Runs 10K through Victoria Park (has Strava, of course)
- **11:30 AM** — Elena suggests visiting the new show at Whitechapel Gallery
- **1:00 PM** — Lunch near gallery. Discusses what they saw. James photographs his favourite pieces
- **2:30 PM** — Wanders into a small project space nearby. Discovers a photographer he's never seen
- **4:00 PM** — At home, googling the photographer. Finds out a print is £400. "That's actually reasonable"
- **6:00 PM** — Dinner party at a friend's flat. Notices their art on the walls. Asks about it. Competitive instinct kicks in
- **10:00 PM** — Shows Elena something on Instagram. "We should go to Frieze this year"

### Current Art Behaviour

**Discovery channels:**
- Elena (primary curator of their shared taste)
- Guardian / FT culture sections
- Instagram (follows galleries, not individual artists)
- Frieze / art fair email newsletters
- Friends' recommendations at dinner parties
- Google Arts & Culture app (occasional)

**Buying behaviour:**
- Bought 5 of 7 pieces on impulse during gallery visits
- Price sensitivity: comfortable up to £1,500 for prints/photos; hesitant above £3,000
- Needs validation: "Do you think this is good?" (asks Elena, then friends)
- Has never researched provenance or artist market trajectory
- Keeps receipts in a drawer; no documentation system

**Pain points:**
- Can't remember which gallery they visited 3 months ago
- Has no record of works they considered but didn't buy
- Embarrassed when friends ask about an artwork on their wall and he can't recall the artist's full background
- Feels like art knowledge is something he "should" have but doesn't
- Competitive FOMO: friends seem to know more about art than he does

### The FOMO Triggers

| Trigger | Emotion | Attesté Response |
|---|---|---|
| Friend mentions seeing an amazing show he missed | "I need to keep up" | **Encounter Log** — at least document what you *do* see |
| Dinner party where everyone discusses art confidently | Inadequacy masked as nonchalance | **AI Sommelier** — feeds him context about his own collection |
| Sees Frieze content on Instagram | "We should be there" | **Fair Companion** — guided fair experience, not just wandering |
| Can't answer Elena's question about a piece they own | Mild embarrassment | **Collection Dashboard** — everything in one place, artist bios included |
| Spotify Wrapped / Strava Year in Review | "I want one of those for art" | **Year in Art** — the original hook for this persona |

### James's Attesté Journey

**Week 1 — Activation**
- Sees a "Year in Art" post shared by a design Twitter account. "We need this."
- Downloads with Elena. Both create profiles
- Completes taste quiz: "Contemporary Eye — You're drawn to photography and mixed media with urban energy"
- Logs their existing collection by uploading photos (free tier allows basic logging)
- **Key moment:** "Wait, we actually have 7 pieces? I thought it was like 4"

**Month 1 — Engagement**
- Logs encounters every weekend gallery visit (12 encounters in first month)
- Discovers the "Gallery Map" feature — finds 3 project spaces near their flat they never knew existed
- Earns "Weekend Warrior" badge (4 consecutive weekend gallery visits)
- Uses AI Art Scout before a weekend: "What's on in Hackney this week that matches our taste?"
- Elena and James compare their individual Taste Profiles — playful debate ensues
- **Key moment:** "This is genuinely useful. It's like Strava for our gallery habit"

**Month 2 — Deepening**
- At a dinner party, James casually pulls up his collection on Attesté: "Oh this piece? Let me show you..."
- Friends are impressed. Two download the app that evening
- Encounters a "Complete your provenance" prompt on his most valuable piece (the original painting)
- Realizes the painting doesn't have proper documentation → feels uneasy
- **Key moment:** "Maybe I should actually be more organized about this"

**Month 3 — Conversion**
- Upgrades to Collector ($9/mo) to unlock:
  - Full provenance chain for existing collection
  - Attesté Certificates for pieces where he has receipts
  - Unlimited AI Sommelier queries
  - CSV export (the data nerd in him loves this)
- Starts building proper records. Contacts the gallery where they bought the painting for documentation
- Fair Companion unlocks just before Frieze: structured walkthroughs, artist context, save-and-compare
- **Key moment:** "£9 a month to actually know what we own and what we like? Obvious"

**Month 6 — Loyalty & Advocacy**
- Collection has grown to 10 pieces (all documented from purchase)
- Year in Art preview: 73 encounters, 28 galleries, favourite artist discovered (a ceramicist from Peckham)
- Shares Year in Art to Instagram, Twitter, and WhatsApp group — 4 friends sign up
- Has become the "art couple" among their friends. Gets asked for gallery recommendations regularly
- Considering upgrading to Premium for Insurance Vault — "our collection is actually worth something now"
- **Key moment:** "Art went from 'a nice Saturday activity' to 'a real part of who we are as a couple'"
