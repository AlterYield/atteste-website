# Attesté FOMO Marketing Plan — Part 1: Platform Strategy

---

## Channel-by-Channel Breakdown

### Overview

Attesté's marketing operates across 7 primary channels, each optimized for specific personas and funnel stages. The strategy is built on a core principle: **art collectors don't respond to traditional SaaS marketing.** They respond to community, credibility, fear of loss, and social proof from peers they respect.

---

## 1. Instagram

### Why It Matters
Instagram is where art lives digitally. It's the primary discovery, research, and social signalling platform for 6 of our 8 consumer personas (C1 Mia, C2 James, C3 Priya, C4 David, C7 Zanele, C8 Thabo). It's also where galleries (B1 Sarah) maintain their public presence.

### Account Strategy

**Handle:** @atteste.art
**Bio:** "Your art collection, beautifully managed. Free to start."
**Link in bio:** Linktree or equivalent with: Sign Up | Features | Stories | Blog

**Content pillars:**

| Pillar | % of Content | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Collection Showcases** | 30% | Carousel, Reels | Show what Attesté looks like in action — beautiful, aspirational collection views. Use beta users' collections (with permission) or create mock collections |
| **Collector Stories** | 25% | Reels, Stories, carousels | "Why I started documenting my collection" — real people, real emotions, real pain points resolved |
| **Art Education** | 20% | Carousels, infographics | "What is provenance and why it matters," "5 signs your collection is underinsured," "How to photograph art properly" |
| **FOMO Triggers** | 15% | Stories, single image posts | Statistics, cautionary tales, urgency messaging (see FOMO Tactics in Part 5) |
| **Behind the Scenes** | 10% | Stories, Reels | Product development, team, values, the mission behind Attesté |

### Paid Strategy

**Budget allocation:** 40% of total digital ad spend

**Campaign types:**

| Campaign | Objective | Audience | Persona Target | Creative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Reach | Interest-based: art, galleries, collecting, interior design, auction houses | C1, C3, C4 | "Your art deserves more than a phone photo" — carousel showing messy phone gallery → Attesté collection view |
| **Consideration** | Traffic | Lookalike audiences from sign-ups + retargeting website visitors | C2, C7, C8 | Feature-focused: provenance, insurance vault, portfolio tracking |
| **Conversion** | Sign-up | Retargeting: visited pricing page, started sign-up, engaged with 3+ posts | All | "Start for free. Upgrade when you're ready." Direct CTA |
| **Social Proof** | Engagement | Broad art-interested audience | C1, C7 | Collector testimonials, collection showcase reveals, milestone celebrations |

**Ad creative rules:**
- Never use stock art images. Always use real artworks (with permission) or Attesté interface screenshots
- Always show the product — not abstract brand imagery
- Lead with the pain point, not the feature
- Mobile-first design (90%+ of art Instagram is consumed on mobile)

### Organic Growth Tactics

1. **Collector Spotlight Series:** Weekly feature of a real collector's Attesté collection. They share it to their audience, we gain their followers
2. **Artist Partnership Posts:** Artists recommend Attesté to their collectors. We feature the artist's work in our feed. Mutual value exchange
3. **Hashtag Strategy:** Own #MyAttesteCollection. Participate in #ArtCollector #ContemporaryArt #AfricanArt #SAArt #ArtCollection
4. **Instagram Guides:** "How to start collecting art," "Documenting your collection," "Understanding provenance" — evergreen, searchable content
5. **Stories Engagement:** Polls ("How do you track your collection?"), quizzes ("Is your collection properly insured?"), countdowns (feature launches)

## 5. Email Marketing

### Why It Matters
Email is the highest-converting channel for SaaS. It nurtures leads, converts free users, retains paying users, and reactivates churned users. Every persona touches email at multiple points in their journey.

### List Segmentation

| Segment | Definition | Primary Content | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-sign-up** | Website visitors who haven't signed up | Educational content, FOMO triggers, social proof | Weekly |
| **Free tier** | Active free users | Feature highlights, upgrade prompts, success stories from upgraded users | 2x/week for first month, then weekly |
| **Free → inactive** | Free users who haven't logged in for 14+ days | Re-engagement: "Your collection is waiting," new feature announcements | Weekly for 4 weeks, then monthly |
| **Standard tier** | Paying Standard users | Advanced features, tips & tricks, Premium upgrade prompts | Weekly |
| **Premium tier** | Paying Premium users | Power user content, early access to new features, community invitations | Bi-weekly |
| **Gallery/Advisor** | B2B users | Business-focused content, case studies, product updates | Bi-weekly |
| **Enterprise** | Corporate clients | Quarterly business reviews, industry reports, product roadmap | Monthly |
| **Churned** | Cancelled users | Win-back: new features, special offers, "what's changed since you left" | Monthly for 3 months |

### Key Email Sequences

**1. Welcome Sequence (7 emails over 14 days)**
- Day 0: Welcome + first steps guide
- Day 1: "Upload your first work" (with tutorial)
- Day 3: "Why provenance matters" (educational + feature highlight)
- Day 5: Social proof — "Meet a collector who catalogued 200 works in a month"
- Day 7: "Is your collection insured?" (FOMO trigger + Insurance Vault feature)
- Day 10: "What you're missing on Standard" (upgrade prompt — soft)
- Day 14: "Ready to get serious?" (upgrade prompt — direct, with limited-time offer)

**2. Upgrade Sequence (5 emails over 10 days — triggered when free user hits limits)**
- Email 1: "You've outgrown Free — here's why collectors upgrade"
- Email 2: Feature comparison (Free vs. Standard vs. Premium)
- Email 3: Collector testimonial — "The moment I upgraded, everything changed"
- Email 4: "What's at risk without proper documentation" (FOMO)
- Email 5: Limited-time upgrade offer (first month free or 20% off annual)

**3. Reactivation Sequence (4 emails over 4 weeks — triggered by 14-day inactivity)**
- Week 1: "Your collection misses you" (gentle, feature reminder)
- Week 2: "3 new features you haven't tried" (product update)
- Week 3: "Other collectors are doing this..." (social proof + FOMO)
- Week 4: "Last chance: exclusive offer to come back" (incentive)

## 6. Partnerships & Referrals

### Why It Matters
Art collecting is a trust-based activity. Peer recommendations and institutional endorsements carry more weight than any ad. The referral channel has the highest conversion rate and the lowest cost per acquisition.

### Partnership Categories

**Tier 1: Gallery Partnerships**
- **Model:** Galleries recommend Attesté to buyers at point of sale. Gallery provides a branded certificate of authenticity that includes an Attesté QR code linking to the work's digital provenance chain
- **Value exchange:** Gallery gets free/discounted platform access for their own inventory management. Attesté gets warm leads at the moment of highest engagement (purchase)
- **Target:** 20 SA galleries in Year 1
- **Conversion estimate:** 15–25% of gallery buyers sign up

**Tier 2: Auction House Partnerships**
- **Model:** Post-sale email from auction house includes Attesté CTA: "Catalogue your new acquisition." Co-branded content on collection management
- **Value exchange:** Auction house adds value to buyer experience. Attesté gets high-intent, high-value users
- **Target:** Strauss & Co, Aspire Art Auctions (SA's two major auction houses)
- **Conversion estimate:** 8–12% of auction buyers engage

**Tier 3: Art Fair Partnerships**
- **Model:** Attesté sponsors collector lounges or VIP experiences at major fairs. Provides "Art Fair Companion" feature: scan works at the fair, save to wishlist, track purchases
- **Value exchange:** Fair enhances collector experience. Attesté gets mass exposure to serious collectors
- **Target:** Investec Cape Town Art Fair, FNB Art Joburg, 1-54 (if expanding internationally)
- **Conversion estimate:** 3–5% of fair attendees sign up

**Tier 4: Professional Referrals**
- **Model:** Art advisors, insurance brokers, estate attorneys, and wealth managers recommend Attesté to their clients
- **Value exchange:** Professional adds value to client relationship. Attesté gets pre-qualified, high-LTV users
- **Target:** 15 professional referral partners in Year 1
- **Conversion estimate:** 30–50% (highest conversion — comes with trusted recommendation)

### Referral Programme (User-to-User)

**Mechanism:**
- Every user gets a unique referral link
- Referrer benefit: 1 month free (Standard/Premium) per successful referral
- Referee benefit: Extended free trial (60 days instead of 30) or first month 50% off
- Referral tracked and credited automatically

**Gamification:**
- "Collection Circle" — share your referral link, see who you've brought to the platform
- Milestone rewards: 3 referrals = 3 months free; 5 referrals = exclusive early access to new features; 10 referrals = annual plan gifted + "Founding Collector" badge
- Leaderboard for top referrers (opt-in, anonymized by default)

**Target:** 30% of new sign-ups from referral within 12 months

Art collectors are sophisticated readers. They consume long-form content, editorial, and expert commentary. Content marketing builds the brand credibility that paid advertising alone cannot achieve.
