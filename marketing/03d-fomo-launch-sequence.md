# Attesté FOMO Marketing Plan — Part 4: Launch Sequence

---

## Pre-Launch, Launch, and Post-Launch Phases

### Launch Philosophy

Attesté does not launch like a SaaS product. It launches like a cultural event. Art collectors respond to exclusivity, community, and curation — not "Product Hunt launches" or "free trial" blasts. The launch creates the feeling that something important is happening, and you either join now or miss the moment.

---

## Phase 1: PRE-LAUNCH (Weeks -12 to -1)

### Objective
Build anticipation, capture a waitlist, seed social proof, and establish credibility before a single user signs up publicly.

### Week -12 to -8: Foundation

**1. Brand & Visual Identity Finalized**
- Logo, colour palette, typography, photography style
- Brand guidelines document for all future content
- Social media templates (Instagram grid, Stories, Twitter/X headers)
- Email templates (welcome, feature, FOMO, B2B)
- Photography: commission a shoot of 3–4 real collections (with permission) using Attesté. These images become launch assets

**2. Website Live (Waitlist Mode)**
- Landing page: headline + value proposition + waitlist sign-up
- Headline options (A/B test):
  - "Your art collection, beautifully managed"
  - "Every work. Every story. One place."
  - "The platform serious collectors have been waiting for"
- Social proof section: "Built for collectors, galleries, and institutions"
- Feature preview: 3–4 key features with visuals (Collection View, Provenance Chain, Insurance Vault, Collection Memoirs)
- Waitlist incentive: "Join the waitlist → Get Founding Collector status (free for life on select features)"

**3. Social Accounts Activated**
- Instagram: @atteste.art — begin posting 3x/week (brand building, art education, teaser content)
- Twitter/X: @attesteart — begin engaging with art market conversations
- LinkedIn: Company page — begin posting thought leadership
- Content is NOT about the product yet. It's about the problem: "How do you track your collection?" "Is your art insured?" "What happens to your collection when you're gone?"

**4. Seed Partnerships**
- Approach 5 pilot galleries for beta testing (Cape Town focus)
- Approach 3 art advisors for beta testing
- Approach 1 corporate collection for enterprise beta
- Approach 5–10 individual collectors for consumer beta

### Week -8 to -4: Beta & Social Proof

**5. Beta Programme (Closed)**
- 50 beta users: 30 consumers + 10 galleries + 5 advisors + 5 corporate/institutional
- Beta users get:
  - Free Premium access for 12 months
  - "Founding Collector" badge (permanent)
  - Direct access to the product team for feedback
  - Agreement to provide a testimonial/case study if satisfied
- Beta metrics tracked: onboarding completion, features used, time-to-value, NPS
- Weekly check-ins with beta users: what works, what doesn't, what's missing

**6. Waitlist Growth**
- Target: 500 waitlist sign-ups before launch
- Tactics:
  - Instagram content: increase to 5x/week with waitlist CTA in every post
  - Twitter/X threads: "The provenance problem in SA art" / "Why your collection needs a digital home" — with waitlist link
  - Gallery partners share waitlist link with their collectors
  - Art advisor partners mention Attesté to clients
  - PR: seed 2–3 preview articles ("A new platform is about to change how SA collectors manage their art")
  - Email: "You're on the waitlist — here's what's coming" drip sequence (bi-weekly, 4 emails)

**7. Content Stockpile**
- Write 8 blog posts (launch month + 2 months ahead)
- Shoot 4 Collector Spotlight videos/Reels (beta users telling their stories)
- Prepare 20 Instagram posts (scheduled for launch week + post-launch)
- Draft 3 PR pitches (launch day, Week 2, Month 2)
- Prepare all email sequences (Welcome, Upgrade, Reactivation)

### Week -4 to -1: Anticipation Building

**8. Countdown Campaign**
- Instagram Stories: daily countdown (30 days, then intensify at 7 days)
- Twitter/X: daily posts building anticipation. Feature reveals every 3 days
- Email to waitlist: "We're almost ready" (Week -4) → "Launch date announced" (Week -2) → "Tomorrow" (Day -1)
- Beta user testimonials released: one per day in the final week
  - Day -7: Gallery owner Sarah: "This replaced my entire admin process"
  - Day -6: Collector Zanele: "For the first time, I know exactly what I have"
  - Day -5: Advisor Michael: "My annual reports now take 5 hours, not 25"
  - Day -4: Collector Richard: "My children will know every story"
  - Day -3: Corporate Nomsa: "From a spreadsheet to a museum-grade system"
  - Day -2: Collector Thabo: "My spreadsheet was lying to me"
  - Day -1: Collector Mia: "I didn't even know I was a collector"

**9. Press Embargo**
- Embargo packages sent to 8–10 media outlets at Week -2
- Includes: press release, high-res images, founder interview availability, beta user stories
- Embargo lifts on launch day at 08:00 SAST

**10. Founding Collector Offer Finalized**
- Available only during launch week (7 days)
- Offer: Sign up during launch week → Founding Collector badge + 3 months Premium free
- Creates urgency: "After launch week, this offer disappears"

## Phase 3: POST-LAUNCH (Weeks 1–12)

### Week 1–4: Activation & Conversion

**Objective:** Convert sign-ups into active users. Move free users toward Standard.

**Key activities:**

1. **Onboarding optimization**
   - Monitor where users drop off in the onboarding flow
   - A/B test onboarding emails (subject lines, content, timing)
   - Personal outreach to high-value sign-ups (large collections, gallery owners, advisors) — offer onboarding assistance

2. **Content shift: from "what is Attesté" to "how to use Attesté"**
   - Instagram: tutorial Reels, tips & tricks carousels
   - Blog: "How to catalogue your first 10 works" / "Setting up your Insurance Vault"
   - Email: feature tutorials triggered by user behaviour (signed up but hasn't uploaded → "Upload your first work in 60 seconds")

3. **Free tier friction optimization**
   - Ensure free tier limit (25 works) is reached naturally within 2–3 weeks of active use
   - When limit is hit, upgrade prompt appears with social proof: "500 collectors have already upgraded"
   - Upgrade email sequence activates automatically

4. **Feedback collection**
   - In-app surveys at Day 7 and Day 30
   - NPS survey at Day 30
   - Direct outreach to 20 most active users for detailed feedback

### Week 4–8: Growth & Social Proof

**Objective:** Build social proof, activate referral programme, expand partnerships.

**Key activities:**

1. **Social proof engine**
   - Weekly Collector Spotlight: real users sharing their Attesté experience
   - Milestone celebrations: "1,000 collectors!" "10,000 works catalogued!" — share across all channels
   - User-generated content: encourage collectors to share their Attesté collection views with #MyAttesteCollection

2. **Referral programme launch**
   - Activate referral links for all users
   - Email announcement: "Share Attesté with a fellow collector — you both win"
   - In-app referral prompt after specific actions (uploading 10th work, sharing first collection)

3. **Partnership expansion**
   - Onboard 10 more galleries (beyond the initial 5 beta)
   - Activate auction house partnership (post-sale email integration)
   - Approach first art fair for sponsorship/partnership

4. **Content marketing ramp-up**
   - First quarterly SA Art Market Report
   - Guest blog posts from gallerists and advisors using Attesté
   - First podcast appearance (founder or content lead)

### Week 8–12: Optimization & Scaling

**Objective:** Optimize conversion funnels, reduce churn, prepare for scale.

**Key activities:**

1. **Funnel optimization**
   - Analyse full conversion funnel: impression → click → sign-up → activation → upgrade → retention
   - Identify and fix the weakest conversion point
   - A/B test pricing page, upgrade prompts, and email sequences

2. **Churn prevention**
   - Identify leading indicators of churn (login frequency drop, no new works added in 30 days)
   - Activate reactivation email sequence for at-risk users
   - Personal outreach to churned Premium users — understand why they left

3. **Paid advertising optimization**
   - Review CAC by channel: shift budget toward highest-performing channels
   - Create lookalike audiences from best-converting user segments
   - Test new ad creatives based on 3 months of data

4. **B2B sales acceleration**
   - Gallery and advisor case studies published
   - Enterprise pipeline development: outreach to 10 SA corporates with known collections
   - First B2B webinar: "Gallery Management in the Digital Age" or "Corporate Collection Best Practices"

5. **Product-led growth features**
   - Shareable collection links (public showcase) — viral loop
   - Gallery buyer certificates with Attesté branding — awareness at point of purchase
   - Artist profiles with links to collectors — cross-pollination

## Launch Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Server overload on launch day | Low | High | Load test at 3x expected traffic. Auto-scaling enabled. Status page ready |
| Critical bug discovered post-launch | Medium | High | Engineering on standby Day 0–3. Hotfix deployment process tested. User communication template ready |
| Low sign-up velocity | Medium | Medium | Reserve paid ad budget for Day 2–3 boost. Activate secondary PR pitches. Extend Founding Collector offer by 3 days |
| Negative media coverage | Low | Medium | Prepare response templates. Founder available for interviews. Fix legitimate concerns immediately and publicly |
| Gallery partner doesn't promote | Medium | Low | Have backup partners. Not dependent on any single partner for launch success |
| Waitlist-to-signup conversion < 30% | Medium | Medium | Immediate email follow-up to non-converters. Survey: "What's holding you back?" Address objections in real-time |
