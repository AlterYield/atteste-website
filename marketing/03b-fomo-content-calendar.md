# Attesté FOMO Marketing Plan — Part 2: Content Calendar

---

## 12-Month Theme & Cadence Plan

### Calendar Overview

Each month has a **primary theme** that unifies content across all channels, a **FOMO angle** that drives urgency, and **persona spotlights** that ensure we're speaking to the full spectrum throughout the year. Art world seasonality (fairs, auctions, academic calendar) is woven into the timing.
